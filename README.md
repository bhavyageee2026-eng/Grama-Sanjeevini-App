🌿 Grama-Sanjeevini App

A smart healthcare support system for rural communities

📌 Project Overview

Grama-Sanjeevini is an Android-based application designed to assist people in rural areas by providing easy access to medical information and nearby medicine availability. In many villages, medical stores are the primary source of healthcare advice, and unavailability of medicines forces villagers to travel long distances. This app aims to reduce that gap by offering a digital solution.

❗ Problem Statement

In remote villages:

Medical stores are the only source of healthcare guidance
Medicines may not always be available
People travel long distances (10–20 km) to find medicines
Lack of proper information causes delays in treatment
💡 Proposed Solution

Grama-Sanjeevini provides:

📍 Nearby medical store availability
💊 Medicine stock information
🧾 Basic health guidance using AI
📱 Simple and user-friendly interface for rural users
🚀 Features
🔍 Search medicines easily
📍 Locate nearby medical shops
🤖 AI-based suggestions for basic health issues
📦 Medicine availability tracking
👤 User-friendly interface
🛠️ Tech Stack
